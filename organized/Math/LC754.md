# 754. Reach a Number

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Topics:** Math, Binary Search  
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reach-a-number/

---

## Approach
The solution starts by taking the absolute value of the target, as the distance to reach a negative target is the same as its positive counterpart. It then iteratively increments a step counter and adds it to a running sum until the sum exceeds or equals the target. Once the sum is greater than or equal to the target, it continues to increment the step counter and add it to the sum until the difference between the sum and the target is even, ensuring that the target can be reached. This approach leverages the properties of triangular numbers to find the minimum steps required.

## Complexity
- Time: O(sqrt(target)) with the number of iterations proportional to the square root of the target due to the properties of triangular numbers.
- Space: O(1) with a constant amount of space used to store the step counter, sum, and target.
